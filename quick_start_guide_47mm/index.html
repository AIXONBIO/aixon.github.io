Quick Start Guide

AIXON EV Isolation Membrane • 47mm
1

Assembly

Place the membrane securely into a compatible filtration apparatus. Ensure tight seal before applying vacuum.

Recommended working vacuum: < 200 mmHg
2

Wetting

Pour 4 mL of PBS onto the membrane and aspirate completely to wet the surface.

3

Loading

Pour sample onto the membrane and aspirate completely.

Recommended loading volume is < 25 mL, subject to sample characteristics.
4

Washing

Pour 2 mL of PBS onto the membrane and aspirate. Repeat this step once more.

5

Recovery

Add 250 μL of PBS. Pipette up and down 20-30 times, then transfer the liquid to a microtube. Repeat this step once more and pool the liquid.

6

Analysis

You should have approx 500 μL of isolated EVs ready for Nanoparticle Tracking Analysis (NTA) or downstream applications.
